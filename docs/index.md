# MediaTrail

[Magyar változat](hu/index.md)

MediaTrail is a private log for what you watch, read, and play. You run it yourself. Each person has their own library: movies, series, seasons, episodes, anime, manga, games, books, comics, and board games.

A title can hold a status, a score, progress, dates, and a note. Series are tracked season by season, and episodes keep their own watches. Lists, a calendar, and a statistics page sit next to the library. The interface is English or Hungarian. The choice is on Preferences, and it applies to that signed-in account. A visitor who is not signed in sees English.

This book is the MediaTrail manual.
